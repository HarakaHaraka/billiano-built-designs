# Complaints Procedure — Billiano Built Designs

We take complaints seriously. A complaint handled well is how we prove the standards we advertise. This procedure applies to all services provided by Billiano Built Designs Ltd.

## How to complain

Raise a complaint by any of the following, whichever suits you:

- Email: enquiries@billianobuiltdesigns.co.uk (subject line "Complaint")
- Phone or WhatsApp: 07943 569024
- Post: [PLACEHOLDER: registered office address]

Tell us what happened, when, the property or project it relates to, and what outcome you are looking for. If you need this procedure in another format or need support making a complaint, tell us and we will accommodate it.

## What happens next

**Stage 1 — Acknowledgement.** We acknowledge your complaint within **2 working days**, confirm who is handling it, and tell you when to expect a full response.

**Stage 2 — Investigation and response.** The director investigates and gives you a full written response within **10 working days**. If the matter is complex and we need longer, we tell you why and give a revised date — never more than 20 working days without your agreement. The response states what we found, what we will do to put it right, and any goodwill or remedial action offered.

**Stage 3 — Escalation.** If you are not satisfied with the Stage 2 response, say so within 14 days and the complaint is reviewed afresh at director level, considering any new information. You receive a final written position within **10 working days** of escalation.

## Work carried out by trade partners

Where your complaint concerns installation or construction work performed by one of our accredited trade partners, we coordinate the complaint with that partner and their certification body (for example Gas Safe or their electrical competent-person scheme), and we remain your single point of contact throughout. You will not be passed back and forth.

## If we cannot resolve it

If our final response does not resolve the matter:

- **Consumers** may refer the dispute to an approved Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) provider; we will confirm the appropriate ADR body for the service in question in our final response. Consumer guidance is also available from Citizens Advice.
- Complaints about work under a certification or grant scheme may be raised with the relevant scheme operator (for example MCS for microgeneration installations, or TrustMark for registered work).
- Nothing in this procedure removes your legal rights.

## Records and learning

We keep a complaints register recording every complaint, its outcome, and any change made as a result. Complaint volumes and themes are reviewed quarterly and inform how we work.
